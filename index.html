How I work

Since 2017 I have worked across digital investigation teams and research labs, tracing coordinated inauthentic behaviour, influence operations, online fraud and platform failures. Based in Bangladesh, my work spans South and Southeast Asia. I co-founded Activate Rights, a research and policy collective working at the intersection of technology, society and justice, where I lead the research, and I teach data and society at Jahangirnagar University.

The work runs from monitoring and open-source analysis through to published investigation: account networks and their coordination, paid and anonymous campaigns, synthetic media and AI-generated content, online fraud, targeted abuse against people in public life, and how platforms handle — or fail to handle — what runs on them across the region’s languages and markets. It stays focused on the people behind the networks, not only the harm they leave, and on what that evidence can be made to do. Alongside the research I build and lead the teams that do it, and train journalists and journalism academics.

Where I have been

2025 —

Co-founder & Research Lead

Activate Rights

Co-founder and Research Lead at a collective working on internet freedom and digital rights in Bangladesh and the wider region. The work runs across six strands — community support on privacy, surveillance and censorship; digital governance policy; internet shutdown monitoring; archiving human rights violations for accountability; localising knowledge; and collective resistance. Projects include Shutdown Watch, an election monitoring situation room, TFGBV research, an internet freedom fellowship, and the Archive & Resist Conclave on community-led documentation and transitional justice. Collaborators include OHCHR, the EU, WITNESS, UNDP, Netra News, The Dissent and Nuthell Today.

2026 —

Adjunct Lecturer

Jahangirnagar University

Graduate course in data journalism, Department of Journalism and Media Studies. One of Bangladesh’s oldest public universities.

Jun 2025 — Sep 2026

Digital Investigation Editor

The Dissent

A digital investigative media outlet based in Dhaka focusing on combating disinformation, foreign influence and investigating cybercrimes, and a project of Fourth Estate Ltd. The post ran under a memorandum of understanding between The Dissent and Activate Rights: capacity development for their newsroom, and joint reporting on foreign information manipulation and interference (FIMI) and coordinated inauthentic behaviour (CIB).

2022 — 2025

Research Coordinator

Digitally Right – Dismislab

Led Dismislab from 2022, a research platform that describes itself as independent, neutral and non-partisan and began as an information laboratory monitoring current affairs, conducting research, verifying information, and building the critical-thinking capacity of journalists and readers. The team publishes original investigations into manipulated information environments, platform manipulation and AI-generated political content — work routinely cited by Bangladeshi and international outlets, including during election periods.

2020 — 2022

Staff Fact-checker

BOOM Bangladesh

BOOM, certified by the Poynter Institute’s International Fact-Checking Network, became Meta’s first third-party partner in Bangladesh in April 2020, with a dedicated team on the ground; it also works in India and Myanmar. Monitored Facebook — the dominant platform in Bangladesh — and investigated claims circulating on it in a language the platform’s automated systems handle poorly.

2017 — 2020

Staff Fact-checker

BD FactCheck

Bangladesh’s first fact-checking initiative, a nonpartisan non-profit founded in 2017 and operated largely through Facebook. Contributed from 2017, covered the 2018 national election, joined full time in early 2019.
